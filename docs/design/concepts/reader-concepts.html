5 · Reader menus: which layout?

Tap the centre of the page to show them. A keeps the old app's layout: a top bar, a floating chapter slider, and a six-button dock. B merges the slider into one bottom panel with five tools. C is the quietest: a slim bottom bar with the slider and four tools, and everything else in the Aa sheet. All are Liquid Glass on the floating layer.

6 · Appearance (Aa) sheet: which style?

A is the old app's four tiles (font, paragraph, screen, theme) that each open a page. B puts the common controls right in the sheet (size, line height, theme swatches, page turn), with a "More" row for the rest. C splits it into tabs.

7 · Page info: how much around the text?

A, like the old app: a header with chapter count and format, and a footer with chapter and percent. B: only a slim footer (chapter · page · time · battery). C: nothing but a hairline progress line at the bottom. Whichever you pick stays switchable in settings.

# Wave 3: Daily reading (TestFlight build 5) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED: run this plan with the project skill
> `running-a-milestone` (`.claude/skills/running-a-milestone/SKILL.md`). Task 1 is the
> controller's contracts commit, Tasks R1–P1 are one build round with each brief following
> the skill's §3, and Task 2 is the merge, check, fix and ship round. Steps use checkbox
> (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Close the gaps KaiC hits every day in build 4: how a book opens from the shelf,
moving through the text, finding a chapter, the screen, illustrations and PDFs. Ship it all
as TestFlight build 5.

**Architecture:**
- One contracts commit lays every shared piece: a v3 schema for reading sessions,
  preferences, demo flags, strings and shot lines, plus stubs with final signatures.
- On top of it, seven agents build in parallel, each owning a disjoint set of files.
- The reader's place-keeping (`ReaderModel`'s jump, hold and land) is not redesigned. New
  movement, such as tap-to-scroll, goes through the same `ScrollPosition` and tracking.

**Tech Stack:**
- SwiftUI on iOS 26+, Swift 5 mode, XcodeGen.
- GRDB, ZIPFoundation and SwiftSoup.
- Apple frameworks: PDFKit, ImageIO, Photos (add-only).
- The Mac loop (`tools/mac/run.sh`), and GitHub Actions for compile and TestFlight.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-31-shiori-design.md`. Sections:
- §5.3: the Continue card.
- §6.2: crest tap and the ribbon.
- §6.5: book detail.

## At a glance (KaiC's one screen)

### 1. Done: phone steps (build 5)

1. **Continue card:** open Shiori. The top of the shelf shows a Continue card with your
   book's cover, title, current chapter and %. Tap Continue and the reader opens on the
   same paragraph.
2. **Detail page:** tap the crest book on the 3D shelf, or any row in the list.
   - The detail page grows out of the cover. It shows:
     - the cover and title
     - the author with a ›
     - the chapter count, the import date and the `SH-` serial
     - a "Reading: <chapter>" card with your progress and reading time
     - the contents
   - Tap 正序 to flip the order.
   - Tap a chapter: the reader opens at it.
   - Tap the author: their books.
   - Tap the cover: it opens large.
3. **Open book directly:** Settings → General → Open book directly → on. Tapping a book now
   opens the reader straight away. Turn it off again.
4. **Tap zones:**
   - Tap the right third of the page: the text moves one screen on, keeping a line of
     overlap.
   - The left third goes back. The centre opens the menus.
   - Kill the app and reopen the book: it opens on the same paragraph.
5. **Screen and gestures:** Aa → Screen and gestures.
   - Right-handed zones, then "Both sides go forward": check how the taps change.
   - Brightness: the reader's slider. Then swipe up and down along the left edge of the
     page, and leaving the reader puts the phone's brightness back.
   - Keep screen awake is on: leave the book open past your auto-lock time and the screen
     stays on.
   - The footer shows the battery.
6. **Contents:**
   - Volumes fold and unfold.
   - Search 3625: chapter 3,625 (第三千六百二十五章) is listed, and tapping it jumps there.
   - The order flips.
7. **Illustrations:** open an EPUB that has illustrations.
   - Pictures show in the text, scaled to the page.
   - Tap one: the viewer opens. Pinch, double-tap, and swipe to the chapter's next picture.
   - Save: iOS asks to add to Photos once, then the picture is in Photos.
8. **PDF:** tap a PDF on the shelf.
   - It opens with its pages, scrolling down by default. You can switch to page by page.
   - There's a thumbnail grid, and Contents when the PDF has an outline.
   - Close and reopen it: same page.
9. **Reading time:** read for five minutes, then open the book's detail page. Reading time
   shows about 5 min.

## Global Constraints

- **Platform:** iOS 26.0 minimum, Xcode 27.1, Swift 5 mode, and no project-level xcconfig.
- **Layout:** never the 440 pt width. Lay out from the available size.
- **Look:**
  - Colours come from tokens, or for reader screens from `ReaderTheme`. Never a literal.
  - Glass only on the floating layer. Radii are 4 / 8 pt, and floating controls are
    capsules.
- **Motion and haptics:**
  - Every animation reads `\.motionReduced` and becomes a short crossfade under it.
  - Haptics only through `.shioriHaptic`.
- **Strings:**
  - Every user-facing string goes in `Localizable.xcstrings` in English and `zh-Hans`.
  - Text built outside views uses `LocalizedStringResource`.
  - An agent lists every new string with its translation in its report.
- **Privacy:**
  - No network.
  - Photos access is add-only (`PHAccessLevel.addOnly`), asked only when Save is tapped.
- **Dependencies:** no new packages.
- **Clean-room:** never open the old app's code. `reference/` is look and feel only.
- **Fixtures:** generated text and generated images only.
- **Comments:** sparse, why-not-what.
- **Ownership:**
  - An agent edits only its **Owns** files and reports anything else it needs.
  - Shared, controller only: `project.yml`, `tools/app-check/Package.swift`, both
    `.xcstrings`, `ios-compile.yml`, `tools/shots.sh`, `tools/mac/*`, `AppModel.swift`,
    `ShioriApp.swift`, `RootView.swift`, `DemoLaunch.swift`, `Tokens.swift`,
    `AppSettings.swift`, `Motion.swift`, `Haptics.swift`, `Icons/ShioriIcon.swift`,
    `Records*.swift`, `LibraryDatabase.swift`, `BookContentSource.swift`,
    `ReaderTypography.swift`, `ReaderPreferences.swift`, `ReaderModel.swift`,
    `ReaderColumn.swift`, `AppearanceSheet.swift` and `Settings/SettingsView.swift`.
- **Agents' gate:**
  - `bash tools/check-app.sh` OK.
  - `swift test` in ShioriCore if touched.
  - `bash tools/check-lib-tests.sh` if ShioriLibrary was touched.
  - Never push, merge or switch branches.
- **Light testing:** tests only where a bug costs KaiC data or his place:
  - tap zones (R1)
  - tap-to-scroll saving the place (R1 UI test)
  - chapter search (R2: a wrong jump loses the place)
  - image sizes (R3)
  - session writes (T1)
  - PDF page restore (P1 UI test)

## Review Focus

1. **A tap-scroll while a jump or hold is landing, or while a chapter joins above.** The
   reader must not save a position it never showed. R1 sends the scroll only when
   `reader.isSettling` is false and the scroll phase is idle. Covered by
   `ReaderTapTests.testTapForwardKeepsPlaceAfterRelaunch`.
2. **An image whose size can't be read** (missing from the archive, SVG, corrupt):
   - its row keeps one fixed height before and after loading, a 4:3 frame with a
     placeholder
   - it never changes height when the data arrives, because that would shift the text
     under the saved paragraph
   - covered by R3's `BookImageTests.unreadableImageHasNoSize`
3. **Brightness left changed:** the reader is closed, the app goes to the background, or a
   sheet covers the reader. The phone's brightness comes back on close and on background,
   and the reader's value is re-applied on return. R1 states which paths it covers in its
   report, and the code review checks it.
4. **PDF progress on a book update.** `BookImporter+Update` remaps progress by chapter key,
   and a PDF has no chapters. P1 must confirm a PDF's progress never goes through that
   remap: PDFs aren't updated in place. If one is, report it rather than fixing it in
   `Lib/`.
5. **The app is killed mid-session.** The tracker loses at most the time since its last
   write and never leaves a half row. T1 writes on every pause and every 60 s of an open
   session. Covered by `ReadingSessionTests.flushWritesRunningSession`.

### Task R1: Reader taps and screen

**Spec:** §7.5's screen options (status bar, battery, keep awake, brightness), and §7.6's tap
zones and left-edge brightness swipe.

- [ ] **`TapZonesTests`** (ShioriCore, write first, watch them fail):
  - `standardThirds`: x = 0.1w → `.back`, 0.5w → `.menu`, 0.9w → `.forward`, at
    y = 0.1h, 0.5h and 0.9h.
  - `rightHandedBackIsTopLeftOnly`: (0.1w, 0.2h) → `.back`; (0.1w, 0.8h) → `.forward`;
    (0.5w, 0.5h) → `.menu`; (0.5w, 0.9h) → `.forward`.
  - `leftHandedMirrors`: for every point p, the right-handed answer at p equals the
    left-handed answer at the mirrored point (w − x).
  - `bothSidesForwardNeverGoesBack`: no point maps to `.back`, and the menu zone is
    unchanged.
- [ ] **Implement `TapZones.action`:**
  - **Standard:** the left third goes back, the right third goes forward, and the centre
    third shows the menu, at full height.
  - **Right-handed:** back is the left third of the top half. The menu is the centre
    third between 25% and 75% of the height. Everything else goes forward.
  - **Left-handed:** the right-handed zones mirrored.
- [ ] **`ReaderView`:**
  - `tap(at:)` maps the point through `TapZones`, in the reader's own frame.
  - **Forward or back** scrolls by the viewport height minus the top and bottom margins
    and minus one line height, so one line stays in view. It uses `position.scrollTo(y:)`
    from the offset that `onScrollGeometryChange` reports.
  - The scroll is animated `.smooth(duration: 0.35)`, and instant under Reduce Motion.
  - Ignore taps while the scroll phase isn't idle or `reader.isSettling` is true.
  - A tap still closes the chrome or the Aa sheet first, as today.
- [ ] **`ReaderScreen`:**
  - **Keep awake:** `isIdleTimerDisabled` is set while the reader is open and active, and
    cleared on close and background.
  - **Brightness:**
    - Read the scene's screen brightness on open.
    - Apply `preferences.brightness` when it isn't nil.
    - Restore the system value on close and background, and re-apply on return.
  - **The left-edge strip:** a vertical drag in the leftmost 24 pt sets brightness. Up is
    brighter, and the full page height covers the whole range. It shows a small glass

### Task R2: Contents

**Spec:** §7.8. The look follows `reference/screens/12-reader-contents-dark.png`:
- a glass sheet with a bold title and round search, order (正序/倒序) and close buttons
- numbered rows, the current row on a soft band with 正在阅读

- [ ] **`ChapterSearchTests`** (ShioriCore, first):
  - `emptyMatchesAll`.
  - `textMatchesTitles`: "转职" matches every title containing it, in book order.
  - `numberMatchesChineseNumeral`: "3625" matches "第三千六百二十五章 …".
  - `numberMatchesPosition`: "12" also matches index 11, listed first.
  - `chineseNumeralQuery`: "十二" matches "第十二章 …".
  - `fullWidthDigits`: "１２" behaves like "12".
- [ ] **Implement `ChapterSearch.matches`:**
  - Normalise full-width digits.
  - A query that is a number (Arabic or Chinese) also matches titles whose 第N章/节/回

## Planner's calls (made here; revisit with a reason)

1. **Tap zones in scroll move a screen.** §7.6 describes pages, and in scroll the same zones
   move a screen less one line. That's the closest equivalent and it stays one-handed.
2. **Right-handed and left-handed zones** are defined as in Task R1. The spec names them
   without shapes.
3. **Illustration height is known before loading,** from the image header read with the
   chapter. A row that grew on load would shift the text under the saved paragraph.
4. **Contents search matches chapter numbers** in Arabic and Chinese numerals. In a
   4,834-chapter book, "go to 3625" is the common search.
5. **The detail page hides Edit** until edit book exists, rather than showing a dead button.
6. **Reading sessions start in this wave** (they were M4a's C5). Daily reading starts now,
   and the detail page's reading time needs them.
7. **The PDF page is stored in `Progress.chapterIndex`,** with no schema change. Its
   `fraction` keeps the shelf's progress line working.
8. **Brightness is the reader's own** when set. Leaving the reader always gives the phone
   its brightness back.
9. **Seven agents,** sized by files. T1 is small, but its files touch nothing else.
